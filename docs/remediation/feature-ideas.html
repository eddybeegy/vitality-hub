Start here

Five with the best value for effort

  1. Personal baselines for HRV and resting HR. Turns raw numbers into "normal for you" and sharpens readiness.
  2. Estimated 1RM and personal records. The set-level data from Hevy is already stored and unused for this.
  3. Adaptive TDEE from intake and weight trend. The single most useful nutrition feature, and both inputs are already here.
  4. Day tags and chart annotations. Cheap to build, and every chart and AI insight becomes easier to explain.
  5. Alerts to your phone. Stale feeds, a high BP reading or a low readiness morning, without opening the app. Build this one last, after the other four (Kieron, 21 Oct).
Recovery and readiness

Make readiness personal and explainable

Readiness is computed on the fly from HRV, sleep and yesterday's strain, against fixed thresholds.

Personal baselines with normal bands

value higheffort Smerged #38

Rolling 7 and 60 day baselines for HRV, resting HR and sleep, shown as a shaded band on charts; readiness scores the deviation from your own normal instead of a population number.

Readiness history and "why it moved"

value higheffort Smerged #47

Store one readiness snapshot per London day with its components, then chart it and show which component drove today's change. Today the score is recomputed and never kept.

Early illness and overreach warning

value higheffort Mmerged #49

Flag two or more mornings of resting HR up and HRV down beyond baseline (plus respiratory rate or wrist temperature if Apple Health supplies them). Often shows a cold a day or two before symptoms.

Sleep debt and timing

value medeffort Sdebt merged #50 · timing merged #66

A 14-day running sleep debt against your sleep target, and bedtime drift, alongside the existing sleep consistency widget.

Training

Use the set-level data from Hevy and Strava

workout_sets already holds weight, reps, HR and distance per set, and exercises carry muscle groups.

Estimated 1RM and personal records

value higheffort Smerged #43

Epley or Brzycki e1RM per exercise over time, rep-range PRs (best 3RM, 5RM, 10RM), and a PR badge on the latest workout card.

Plateau detection and overload suggestions

value higheffort Mmerged #52, #78 · Hevy push live

Spot exercises with no e1RM gain in four weeks and propose the next targets; the existing apply-progression route can push them back to the Hevy routine. #78: each stalled lift now gets an Apply to Hevy button. The prescription comes from the last logged session (every routine_exercises.target_weight is NULL), deloading each set to ~90% rounded to the nearest 2.5 kg; it refuses rather than guesses when the lift has no weighted sets, no routine, or sits in several routines.

Weekly sets per muscle group

value medeffort Smerged #51

Hard sets per muscle per week against a target range (for example 10 to 20), extending the muscle heatmap from "recent" to "enough".

Training load: fitness, fatigue and form

value higheffort Mmerged #53

Daily load from Strava HR or duration plus Hevy volume, with 7-day and 42-day rolling loads and their ratio. Feeds readiness and gives a clear "deload now" signal.

Cardio trends from Strava

value medeffort Mmerged #54, #64 · walks only; Strava is a mock

Pace at a fixed heart rate over time, and VO2 max alongside it, so aerobic fitness shows up even when routes vary. #64: no source has ever populated workouts.heart_rate_avg, so the card was empty; the average is now derived from the heart_rate metrics inside each session. Shows 4 walks. The 12 hevy rowing sessions are excluded on purpose — their duration covers the whole mixed session, not the rowing.

Nutrition and body composition

Close the loop between intake and the scale

Cronometer intake and Withings weight and lean mass sit side by side but are never combined.

Adaptive TDEE

value higheffort Mmerged #45

Estimate true daily expenditure from the smoothed weight trend and logged intake over 2 to 4 weeks, then compare it with Apple's active plus basal energy.

Smoothed weight trend and goal date

value higheffort Smerged #55

An exponential moving average line under the daily weigh-ins and a projected date for the weight goal, added to the existing goal progress widget.

Targets that adjust themselves

value medeffort Mmerged #56 · suggestions only

Weekly calorie target suggestion from adaptive TDEE and the goal rate, and protein set per kg of Withings lean mass. Builds on Smart Adjustments; needs adaptive TDEE first.

Training-day and rest-day targets

value medeffort Smerged #60

Two sets of carb and calorie targets, picked automatically from whether a workout is logged or planned that day.

Weekly micronutrient gaps

value loweffort Smerged #57 · food suggestions not done

Which nutrients averaged under 70% of target this week, with the foods you already eat that would close the gap.

Insights and AI

Give the coach better context, and you better questions

AI insights are generated from a context bundle; there is no way to mark what happened on a day.

Day tags and chart annotations

value higheffort Smerged #46

Quick tags such as ill, travel, alcohol, late meal, poor sleep environment, shown as markers on every chart and passed to the AI.

Vitality Hub as an MCP server

value higheffort Mmerged #67, #68

Expose read-only tools (metrics, workouts, nutrition, readiness) so Claude can answer questions over your real data. The MCP SDK is already a dependency and service tokens already exist.

Correlation explorer

value medeffort Mmerged #58

Pick any two metrics with a lag (for example caffeine after 2pm against deep sleep) and see the relationship. An enable_correlations setting exists but nothing uses it.

Weekly and monthly digest

value medeffort Mmerged #69

A Sunday summary of training, sleep, intake and weight against the previous week, delivered by the same channel as alerts.

Period comparison

value medeffort Smerged #59, #76 · prev / 1mo / 1yr baselines

Overlay this week against last, or this month against the same month last year, on any chart.

Health records

Things a GP would ask about

Medications live as a JSON list in settings; there is nowhere for lab results.

Medication and supplement log

value medeffort Mmerged #70, #71

A daily "taken" check per item, adherence over time, and start and stop dates drawn as markers on charts to see effects.

Blood test results

value medeffort Mmerged #72, #73

Manual entry of lipids, HbA1c, ferritin, vitamin D and so on, with reference ranges and trend lines.

Blood pressure categories and averages

value medeffort Smerged #61

NICE home-reading bands and a 7-day average in the style of a home BP protocol. Best done after open item 21 sorts out the BP widget.

Alerts, data and app

Reach you, and keep the data trustworthy

There is no notification channel, and the service worker clears caches but serves nothing offline.

Alerts to your phone

Data coverage calendar

value medeffort Smerged #74

One row per source, one cell per day, coloured by rows received. Gaps like the stalled Apple workouts feed become visible at a glance.

Scheduled backups

Progress photos
